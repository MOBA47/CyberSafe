BUILD YOUR KNOWLEDGE

How to Stay Safe Online

Follow these simple practices to protect yourself online.

🔐

Protect Your Accounts

Use strong, unique passwords and enable two-factor authentication whenever available.

🔗

Check Before Clicking

Avoid suspicious links, unknown downloads, and messages that ask for personal information.

👤

Protect Your Privacy

Do not share sensitive personal information with strangers or untrusted websites.

📱

Update Your Devices

Keep your phone, computer, applications, and security software updated.

🧠

Think Before Sharing

Check information from reliable sources before believing or sharing it online.

🤝

Be Respectful

Treat others with respect and report cyberbullying or harmful online behavior.

KNOW THE RISKS

Common Online Threats

Understanding these threats can help you avoid them.

🎣

Phishing

Fake emails, messages, or websites designed to trick people into giving away private information.

💸

Online Scams

Fraudulent offers or messages that try to steal money or personal information.

💬

Cyberbullying

Using digital platforms to harass, threaten, embarrass, or hurt another person.

📰

Misinformation

False or misleading information shared online that can confuse or mislead people.
